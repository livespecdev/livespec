# Todos Are Private by Default

A todo belongs to the user who created it. Sharing is an explicit, deliberate act — never an emergent property of being on the same team, account, or device.

## Why

The capture surface for personal todos must feel safe enough to record anything — health appointments, gift ideas, half-formed thoughts. The moment a user worries about visibility, they switch back to paper or a private notes app, and we lose the goal of being a trusted single source.

## Implications

- Adding a todo never silently exposes it to anyone else.
- Joining a shared list never reveals the user's personal lists.
- Any future "team" or "workspace" abstraction MUST preserve this invariant.
