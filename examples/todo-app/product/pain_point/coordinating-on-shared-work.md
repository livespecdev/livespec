# Coordinating on Shared Work

Small groups (households, project teams, volunteer crews) need to share a list of things to do, agree on who does what, and know when something is done — without status meetings or chat-thread archaeology.

## Why other tools fail

Chat-based coordination relies on memory and scroll-back. Spreadsheets work but require everyone to learn one team's columns. Project management tools are overkill and assume a workflow most informal groups don't have.
