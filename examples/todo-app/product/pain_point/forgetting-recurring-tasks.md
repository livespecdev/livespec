# Forgetting Recurring Tasks

People who try to keep recurring tasks (weekly trash day, monthly rent, quarterly tax filings) in their head inevitably forget some, and the cost of forgetting ranges from minor annoyance to real financial or social consequence.

## Why other tools fail

Generic reminder apps surface recurring tasks but treat them identically to one-shots: a daily 8am ping for "take out the trash" disappears once dismissed, with no record of whether the task actually got done. Calendar apps require manual entry per occurrence. Paper lists never recur.
