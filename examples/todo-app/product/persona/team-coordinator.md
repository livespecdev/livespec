# Team Coordinator

Person who organizes shared work across a small group — a household, a project team, a club. Creates lists, invites others to them, and tracks completion across people.

## Context

The team coordinator is the de-facto "operations person" in their group. The pain they live with daily is described by [[coordinating-on-shared-work]]: chat threads, spreadsheets, and notes apps that weren't designed for collaboration — leading to duplicate work, forgotten tasks, and unclear ownership.

## What success looks like

- Create a shared list and invite collaborators without friction.
- See at a glance who is doing what on a shared list.
- Get notified when a teammate completes (or misses) a task they care about.
