# Solo Organizer

Person who uses the app to manage their own personal todos — errands, recurring chores, work tasks they own end-to-end. Does not collaborate with others inside the app.

## Context

The solo organizer has tried paper notebooks, sticky notes, and the default OS reminders app, but lacks a single durable place for "everything I need to remember". Their dominant frustration is the one captured by [[forgetting-recurring-tasks]]. Lives mostly on mobile, occasionally on desktop. Values quick capture above all else.

## What success looks like

- Capture a new todo in under three seconds, from anywhere.
- Trust the app to remind them at the right time without having to check it manually.
- Never lose a todo to sync failure or accidental tap.
