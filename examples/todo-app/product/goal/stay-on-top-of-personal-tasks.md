# Stay on Top of Personal Tasks

Help an individual keep an accurate, trusted, low-effort record of everything they need to do, so nothing important slips through the cracks.

## How we measure this

- A user who has been active for at least 30 days holds an average of >20 active todos in the app — indicating they trust it as their primary capture surface, not a scratchpad.
- Fewer than 5% of due todos are dismissed without being marked done — indicating reminders fire at trustworthy moments.
