# Coordinate Team Work

Help a small group share a single source of truth for "what needs to get done", with clear ownership and minimal coordination overhead.

## How we measure this

- A shared list with ≥2 active collaborators sees a completion event from more than one user per week — indicating real collaboration, not solo use with audience.
- Less than 10% of shared todos are completed by someone other than the assignee — indicating ownership is respected, not chaos.
